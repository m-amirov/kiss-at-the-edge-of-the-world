# Art Bible — «Поцелуй на краю света»

## Locked product invariants

The art supports a contemporary romantic road story in Iceland. It must not change the 12-episode topology, the three route cores, the independent ending, or the state/choice model in `docs/SEASON_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Visual language

- Cinematic semi-realistic illustration with restrained brush texture; no photobashing into inconsistent character renders.
- Palette: basalt charcoal, Atlantic blue, moss green, cloud grey, warm amber window light, muted wine accent for Alice's scarf.
- Light: low autumn sun, diffuse overcast daylight, practical warm interiors. Aurora is an optional low-frequency night accent, never a permanent neon backdrop.
- Composition: faces, hands, and meaningful props stay in a central mobile-safe zone; backgrounds reserve lower contrast behind dialogue.
- Emotion: subtle eye direction, posture and distance carry intimacy; avoid exaggerated anime reaction faces that break the grounded travel tone.
- Wardrobe and props remain continuous through the route; weather changes may add layers but do not reset identities.

## Prohibited patterns

- Temporary gradients, colored geometry, text labels, or generic AI portraits as final production assets.
- Neon cyberpunk palettes, fantasy/mystical motifs, unexplained disasters, or tourist-poster collage as the primary visual language.
- Face/wardrobe drift between expressions, invented logos, unreadable generated text, extra fingers, or romantic framing that erases Alice's agency.
- Crops that hide the speaker's expression, cover touch targets, or make mobile dialogue unreadable.
