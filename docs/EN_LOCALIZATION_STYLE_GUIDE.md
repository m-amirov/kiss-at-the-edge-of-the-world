# English Literary Localization Style Guide

English must read as an originally authored contemporary romantic visual novel, not as a line-by-line Russian calque. Preserve plot, chronology, route logic, consent, choice meaning, romantic intensity, factual Icelandic geography, and every structural identifier.

- **Alice:** observant and intelligent, with dry humor; emotionally precise rather than melodramatic.
- **Eric:** concise and practical; warmth is understated and shown through attention and action.
- **Nick:** playful and quick, gradually allowing sincerity to replace performance.
- **Damir:** restrained, with familiar intimacy and the weight of shared emotional history.
- Prefer natural contemporary English syntax, contractions, and dialogue rhythm where they preserve intent.
- Preserve subtext instead of mirroring Russian word order. Do not sanitize or intensify meaning.
- Keep Alice, Eric, Nick, and Damir unchanged. Preserve Icelandic names, spelling, and geography, including Þingvellir where authored.
- Choice labels must retain exact decision semantics and degree of commitment. Technical IDs, route codes, state predicates, and backtick expressions are never translated.
- Cold-read for pronouns and references, tense, voice drift, accidental Cyrillic, geographic corruption, and consistency across conditional alternatives.
